 - Connections is a game Stanislaus and Quiton made to highlight the realistic experiences of networking. -
 <br>  Some people aren't used to networking, while others are too scared to walk up to someone and say hi.
<br>
   That's why we made this game. We wanted to make a game that showed how networking helps, and we wanted to give people the courage to find their voice.
<br>   My brother gave me the courage to find my own voice. We want to be that influence that helps you find yours.
 <br>  We plan on adding more features, like more levels with different goals or challenges, to better represent how useful networking can be due to its diversity.
 <br>  Quiton and I also made sure that some levels could be solved in different ways to showcase how networking doesn't require one path or solution.
<br>
 <br>    We hoped you enjoyed playing the game! This is the first time I've used github for something other than Turbowarp's Unpackager, so please give us tips on improvement! We didn't focus on making the "coolest" or "winning-idea" game. Rather, we wanted to make a way to help people find the courage to speak up and meet new people.
